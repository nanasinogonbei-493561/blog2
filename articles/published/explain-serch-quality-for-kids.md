<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>インターネットで調べるときは、まず知りたいことを一文にします。大事な言葉を選んで検索（けんさく）し、答えが出なければ言葉を変えます。答えが見つかったら、別の資料でも確かめます。</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>「タンポポの綿毛（わたげ）はどうして飛ぶの？」を例に、調べてみましょう。<a href="https://zenn.dev/nanashinogonbei/articles/how-to-improve-search-quality" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">もとになった記事「検索の質を上げるためのキーワード選びと検索の進め方」（Zenn）</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 1. 知りたいことを一文にする"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. 知りたいことを一文にする</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":111} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-111" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-01-question.webp" alt="机の上のタンポポを観察し、綿毛が飛ぶ理由を考える子ども"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>図書室で「タンポポの本が見たい」と言うと、育て方の本も見つかります。「綿毛が飛ぶ理由を知りたい」と伝えれば、どの本を開くか決めやすくなります。インターネットでも、質問を一つ決めると、読むページを選びやすくなります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>この例では、綿毛が何を運ぶか、なぜ風で運ばれるかを、自分の言葉で説明できたら調べ終わりです。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 2. 検索キーワードを選ぶ"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. 検索キーワードを選ぶ</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":112} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-112" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-02-keywords.webp" alt="タンポポ、綿毛、風を表す絵カードを、パソコンのそばに並べる手"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索に使う言葉を「キーワード」といいます。今回は、調べたいものを表す「タンポポ」「綿毛」と、その動きを表す「飛ぶ」を選びます。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>言葉の間を空けて <code>タンポポ 綿毛 飛ぶ</code> と入れてみましょう。育て方を調べるなら、<code>タンポポ 育て方</code> が出発点です。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>質問をそのまま入れても検索できます。短い言葉に分けておくと、検索し直すときに一つずつ変えやすくなります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 3. 検索結果に合わせて言葉を一つ変える"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. 検索結果に合わせて言葉を一つ変える</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":113} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-113" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-03-adjust.webp" alt="花の写真が並ぶ検索結果と、絵カードを足したり外したりした後の結果"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索して出てきたページの一覧を「検索結果」といいます。読みたいページが見つからなければ、結果を見てどの言葉を変えるか決めます。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>話が広すぎるときは、言葉を足します。</strong> <code>タンポポ 綿毛</code> で写真や工作のページが多ければ、「飛ぶ」を足して <code>タンポポ 綿毛 飛ぶ</code> にします。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>条件が細かすぎるときは、言葉を外します。</strong> <code>タンポポ 綿毛 飛ぶ 春</code> で役立つページが少なければ、今の質問にいらない「春」を外して <code>タンポポ 綿毛 飛ぶ</code> に戻します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>別の呼び方を見つけたら、その言葉に入れ替えます。</strong> 開いたページで綿毛を「冠毛（かんもう）」と呼んでいたら、<code>タンポポ 冠毛 飛ぶ</code> を試します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google検索では、<code>"タンポポの綿毛"</code> のように <code>&quot;</code> で囲むと、その言い方を含むページを探せます。<code>タンポポ 綿毛 site:env.go.jp</code> なら、環境省のサイトにしぼれます。<code>site:</code> と <code>env.go.jp</code> の間は空けません。見つからなければ、サイトの指定を外して探し直します。<a href="https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/2466433?hl=ja" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Google検索ヘルプ「Google 検索の結果を絞り込む」</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 4. 見つけた答えを、別の資料でも確かめる"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. 見つけた答えを、別の資料でも確かめる</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":114} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-114" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-04-check.webp" alt="タンポポの綿毛について、画面の説明と図鑑の絵を見比べる子ども"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索結果に短い説明やAIのまとめが出たときも、元のページを開きます。本文を読み、次の三つを確かめましょう。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>自分の質問に答えていますか。</strong> 今回なら、綿毛が飛ぶ理由を書いたところを探します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>だれが書き、何をもとに説明していますか。</strong> 書いた人や団体の名前と、観察や実験などの手がかりを見ます。開館時間など、変わる情報は日付も見ます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>別の資料でも同じことが分かりますか。</strong> 図書室の図鑑や大学・博物館のページも見ます。説明が違うときは、同じ種類や場所について書いてあるかを確かめます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>九州大学と日本植物生理学会の資料では、どちらもタンポポのたねが風で運ばれることを確かめられます。<a href="https://www.forest.kyushu-u.ac.jp/2024/4105" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">九州大学の演習林「たんぽぽ」</a>、<a href="https://jspp.org/hiroba/q_and_a/detail.html?id=4706" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">日本植物生理学会「たんぽぽの綿毛は飛んでも『綿毛』？」</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>タンポポの綿毛はパラシュートのように空気を受け、下についている小さな実が落ちるのをおそくします。</strong> 実の中にはたねがあります。実がゆっくり落ちる間に、風がそれを運びます。<a href="https://www.mech.utsunomiya-u.ac.jp/fel/tanpopo.html" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">宇都宮大学 流体工学研究室「タンポポ」</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 5. 自分の疑問で一回やってみる"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. 自分の疑問で一回やってみる</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":115} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-115" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-05-practice.jpg" alt="質問、絵カード、やり直しの矢印、本の印をノートに描く子ども"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>「月はどうして形が変わって見えるの？」なら、<code>月 形 変わる</code> で検索してみましょう。開いたページに「満ち欠け（みちかけ）」と書いてあれば、その言葉を使って探し直せます。別の資料で答えを確かめたら、分かったことを自分の言葉で一文にし、見た資料の名前をメモします。次は、最近ふしぎに思ったことを一つ調べてみましょう。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>この探し方は、行ける場所や日を手がかりにイベントを探すときにも使えます。<a href="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/2026/09/20/theater-ticket-search/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">小劇場の公演をチケット販売サイトから探す方法</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group --></section>
<!-- /wp:tab-panel -->

<!-- wp:tab-panel {"label":"English","metadata":{"name":"English article"}} -->
<section role="tabpanel" tabindex="0" class="wp-block-tab-panel"><!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: Introduction"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>When you look something up online, start by writing your question in one sentence. Search for the important words and change them if you don’t find an answer. When you find one, check it with another source.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Let’s try this with one question. Why does dandelion fluff fly? <a href="https://zenn.dev/nanashinogonbei/articles/how-to-improve-search-quality" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Original article on choosing keywords and improving your searches (Zenn, in Japanese)</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 1. Put your question into one sentence"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Put your question into one sentence</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":111} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-111" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-01-question.webp" alt="A child studies a dandelion on a desk and wonders why its fluff flies"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you ask for a book about dandelions at the library, you might find one about how to grow them. If you say you want to know why the fluff flies, it’s easier to pick a book. A clear question also helps you choose which web pages to read.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You’re done when you can explain in your own words what the fluff carries and why the wind can carry it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 2. Choose your search keywords"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Choose your search keywords</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":112} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-112" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-02-keywords.webp" alt="Hands arrange picture cards showing a dandelion, fluff, and wind beside a laptop"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The words you use to search are called keywords. Here, “dandelion” and “fluff” name what you’re looking up, and “fly” tells you what it does.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Type <code>dandelion fluff fly</code>, with spaces between the words. To learn how to grow dandelions, start with <code>dandelion how to grow</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can also search for the whole question. A few short words are easier to change one at a time when you search again.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 3. Look at the results and change one word"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Look at the results and change one word</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":113} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-113" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-03-adjust.webp" alt="Flower pictures in search results before and after adding or removing picture cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Search results are the pages your search turns up. If you can’t find a useful page, look at the results to decide which word to change.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Add a word if the results cover too many topics.</strong> If <code>dandelion fluff</code> mostly turns up pictures or crafts, add “fly” to get <code>dandelion fluff fly</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Remove a word if your search is too narrow.</strong> If <code>dandelion fluff fly spring</code> turns up few useful pages, drop “spring” and try <code>dandelion fluff fly</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Swap in a different name if you find one.</strong> If a page calls the tuft of hairs a “pappus,” try <code>dandelion pappus fly</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On Google, put double quotation marks around a phrase, as in <code>"dandelion fluff"</code>, to find pages with that exact phrase. For a Japanese example, <code>タンポポ 綿毛 site:env.go.jp</code> looks for dandelion fluff on Japan’s Ministry of the Environment website. Keep <code>site:</code> and <code>env.go.jp</code> together. If you can’t find what you need, search again without the site restriction. <a href="https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/2466433?hl=en" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Google Search Help on refining searches</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 4. Check the answer with another source"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. Check the answer with another source</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":114} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-114" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-04-check.webp" alt="A child compares a picture in a nature book with information about dandelion fluff on a screen"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the results show a short preview or an AI summary, open the original page too. Read it and ask these three questions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Does it answer your question?</strong> Here, look for the part that explains why the fluff flies.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Who wrote it, and how do they know?</strong> Look for the author or group and any observations or experiments they describe. Check the date when the information can change, as with a museum’s opening hours.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Can you check it somewhere else?</strong> Try a library book or a page from a university or museum. If the explanations differ, check whether they’re about the same kind of plant or the same place.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kyushu University and the Japanese Society of Plant Physiologists both explain that wind carries dandelion seeds. <a href="https://www.forest.kyushu-u.ac.jp/2024/4105" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Kyushu University Forests guide to dandelions (in Japanese)</a> and <a href="https://jspp.org/hiroba/q_and_a/detail.html?id=4706" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Japanese Society of Plant Physiologists Q&amp;A on dandelion fluff (in Japanese)</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Dandelion fluff acts like a parachute and slows the fall of the tiny fruit underneath.</strong> The fruit holds the seed. As it falls slowly, the wind carries it away. <a href="https://www.mech.utsunomiya-u.ac.jp/fel/tanpopo.html" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Utsunomiya University Fluid Engineering Laboratory page on dandelions (in Japanese)</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 5. Try it with a question of your own"},"layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. Try it with a question of your own</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","id":115} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img class="wp-image-115" src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/search-keywords-for-kids-05-practice.jpg" alt="A child draws a question, picture cards, a retry arrow, and a book in a notebook"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Why does the Moon look like it changes shape? Start with <code>moon shape changes</code>. If a page uses the words “Moon phases,” try searching for those words. After checking the answer with another source, write a sentence in your own words and note the names of the sources you used. Then try the same approach with a question of your own.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This also works when looking for events you can attend. Use the place and date as clues. <a href="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/2026/09/20/theater-ticket-search/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">How to find small theater shows on ticket sites</a></p>
