<p>「壊れた冷蔵庫を正しく処分したい」と思って、まずは「冷蔵庫」と検索する。すると通販サイトや新製品の比較ページばかり並び、どのページを開けばいいか迷うことがあります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>そんなときは、<mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">質問の中から大事な言葉を選び、出てきたページを見ながら1つずつ変える</mark>のがコツです。</strong> この記事では、質問の1文づくりから、除外やサイト指定などの絞り込み記号、見つけた答えを公的機関などの一次情報で確かめるところまでを、日常の疑問に沿って整理します。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 1. 知りたいことを1文にする"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. 知りたいことを1文にする</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":231,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/01-question-v2.jpg" alt="奥の冷蔵庫を見ながら、ノートとパソコンを前に処分方法の疑問を整理する大人のイラスト" class="wp-image-231" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>「冷蔵庫について調べたい」だけでは、買い替え向けのレビューも、電気代を抑えるコツも候補に入ります。処分手続きを知りたいなら、ルールと依頼先を説明した公式案内が目的です。<mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">先に質問を1文にしておく</mark>と、読むページを選ぶ手がかりになります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>今回のゴールは、「壊れた冷蔵庫をルールに沿って正しく処分するには、どこに依頼して何が必要か」を自分で説明できる状態です。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 2. 検索キーワードを選ぶ"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. 検索キーワードを選ぶ</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":232,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/02-keywords-v2.jpg" alt="冷蔵庫・リサイクル・チェックリストの絵カードをパソコンの横に並べる大人の手元のイラスト" class="wp-image-232" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索に使う言葉を「キーワード」と呼びます。「壊れた冷蔵庫を正しく処分する方法と必要な手続きを知りたい」から拾うなら、<mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">対象を表す「冷蔵庫」と、目的を表す「処分」「方法」</mark>の3つです。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索欄には、言葉の間をスペースで空けて <code>冷蔵庫 処分 方法</code> と入力します。同じ冷蔵庫でも、故障の原因を調べたいなら <code>冷蔵庫 冷えない 確認</code> のように目的に合わせて変えます。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>質問文をそのまま入力しても検索できますが、短いキーワードに分けておくと、結果を見ながら1語ずつ入れ替えやすくなります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">目的別のキーワード組み合わせ例</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>最初に入れる語が広すぎると、通販ページやニュースなど目的と違う記事が混ざりやすくなります。場面ごとの組み替え例は次のとおりです。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">調べたい場面</th><th scope="col">広すぎる検索語</th><th scope="col">目的に合うキーワード例</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>壊れた家電をルールどおり処分したい</td><td><code>冷蔵庫</code></td><td><code>冷蔵庫 処分 方法</code></td></tr><tr><td>公的な手続きや必要書類を知りたい</td><td><code>パスポート</code></td><td><code>パスポート 更新 必要書類</code></td></tr><tr><td>家電や機器の不具合を確かめたい</td><td><code>冷蔵庫 故障</code></td><td><code>冷蔵庫 冷えない 確認</code></td></tr><tr><td>休日に近場の舞台やイベントを探したい</td><td><code>舞台 公演</code></td><td><code>大阪 小劇場 来月 公演</code></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<!-- /wp:html --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 3. 検索結果に合わせて言葉を1つ変える"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. 検索結果に合わせて言葉を1つ変える</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":233,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/03-adjust-v2.jpg" alt="通販結果の列にキーワードカードを足して公式案内に絞る様子と、絞り込みを外す様子を比べるイラスト" class="wp-image-233" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>検索して表示されたページの一覧が「検索結果」です。目当てのページが見当たらないときは、どんなページが並んでいるかを見ると、次に変える言葉が見つかります。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>通販サイトや製品紹介ばかりなら、言葉を足す。</strong> <code>冷蔵庫</code> だけに「処分」「方法」を加えて <code>冷蔵庫 処分 方法</code> にすると、手放す手順を扱ったページへ絞れます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>条件が細かすぎて役立つページが少ないなら、言葉を外す。</strong> <code>冷蔵庫 処分 無料 即日</code> では民間の広告ばかりになるため、「無料」「即日」を外して <code>冷蔵庫 処分 方法</code> へ戻します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>制度や公式の呼び方を見つけたら、入れ替える。</strong> 検索結果に「家電リサイクル法」という言葉が出てきたら、次の検索を <code>冷蔵庫 家電リサイクル法 処分</code> に変えます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google検索で、ある言い回しをそのまま含むページを探すには、<code>"家電リサイクル法"</code> のように <code>&quot;</code> で囲みます。環境省のサイトだけを探すなら、<code>冷蔵庫 処分 site:env.go.jp</code> と入力します。よく使う絞り込み記号（検索演算子）は次の4つです。 <a href="https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/2466433?hl=ja" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Google検索ヘルプ「Google 検索の結果を絞り込む」</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">よく使うGoogle検索の絞り込み記号（演算子）4選</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">記号</th><th scope="col">できること</th><th scope="col">入力例</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>"語句"</code></td><td>囲んだ言葉がそのままの順番で入るページを探す</td><td><code>"家電リサイクル法" 冷蔵庫</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>-除外語</code></td><td>通販や広告など、見たくない言葉を結果から外す</td><td><code>冷蔵庫 処分 -通販 -買取</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>site:ドメイン</code></td><td>指定したサイトや公的機関の中だけを探す</td><td><code>冷蔵庫 処分 site:env.go.jp</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>filetype:pdf</code></td><td>公式の案内書や手続き資料（PDF）に絞る</td><td><code>家電リサイクル 処分 filetype:pdf</code></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark style="background-color:#fef08a;color:#422006" class="has-inline-color">記号（<code>site:</code>、<code>filetype:</code>、<code>-</code>）は必ず半角で入力し、記号の直後にスペースを入れない</mark>のがポイントです。<mark style="background-color:#fee2e2;color:#991b1b" class="has-inline-color"><code>site: env.go.jp</code> や <code>- 通販</code> のようにスペースを空けると、絞り込みが正しく働きません。</mark> 目当てのページが出ないときは、付けた記号を1つ外して探し直します。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"ja: 4. 見つけた答えを、別の資料でも確かめる"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. 見つけた答えを、別の資料でも確かめる</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":234,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/04-check-v2.jpg" alt="パソコンとタブレットに並んだ2つの公式案内を虫眼鏡とメモで見比べる大人のイラスト" class="wp-image-234" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark style="background-color:#fee2e2;color:#991b1b" class="has-inline-color">検索結果の短い説明やAIのまとめだけで答えを決めないこと。</mark> 元のページを開いたら、次の3つを確かめます。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>知りたいことが書いてあるか：</strong> 今回なら、冷蔵庫を手放す具体的な手順と必要な費用が説明されているかを見ます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>だれが、何をもとに書いたか：</strong> 行政機関や制度の運営団体など、発信元を確認します。<mark style="background-color:#fef08a;color:#422006" class="has-inline-color">料金や受付窓口のように変わりうる情報なら、更新時期も確かめておく</mark>と安心です。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>ほかの一次資料にも同じことが書いてあるか：</strong> 複数の公的ページや自治体の案内を見比べます。説明が違うときは、家庭用か業務用かなど前提条件の差を確認します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>環境省と経済産業省、さらに一般財団法人 家電製品協会（家電リサイクル券センター）の案内を読み比べると、対象品目と正規の処分ルートが共通しています。 <a href="https://www.env.go.jp/recycle/kaden/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">環境省「いらなくなった家電製品は正しくリユース・リサイクル！」</a>、<a href="https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/kaden_recycle/fukyu_special/index.html" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">経済産業省「家電リサイクル特設ページ」</a>、<a href="https://www.rkc.aeha.or.jp/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">一般財団法人 家電製品協会「家電リサイクル券センター」</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>では、どう処分するのが正しいのでしょう。<strong><mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">家庭用の冷蔵庫は家電リサイクル法の対象であり、買い替え先の販売店・購入したお店に引き取りを依頼するか、自治体の案内・指定引取場所への持ち込みで処分し、リサイクル料金と収集運搬料金がかかります。</mark></strong> また、環境省は「無料回収」をうたう無許可の不用品回収業者を利用しないよう注意喚起しています。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">一次情報とまとめ記事の見分け方</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>同じテーマの解説記事が並ぶときは、最後に判断の根拠を一次情報へ戻して確かめます。確認する目印は次の3つです。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>発信元のURL（ドメイン）を見る：</strong>国の機関なら <code>.go.jp</code>、自治体なら都道府県や市区町村の公式ドメイン、公式団体やメーカーなら運営元ページかを確認します。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>更新日と対象条件を見る：</strong>手数料や窓口は改定されることがあります。記事が書かれた年や、家庭用・業務用といった前提条件が自分の状況と合うかを見ます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>広告・PRや出典の有無を見る：</strong>特定の業者やサービスへの申し込みに誘導するページは、条件が偏っていないか一次資料の記載と照らし合わせます。</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. 自分の疑問で1回やってみる</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":235,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/05-practice-v2.jpg" alt="ノートに要点の1文と参照元の印を書き留めて検索手順を振り返る大人のイラスト" class="wp-image-235" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>たとえば「パスポートを更新するにはどんな書類が要るのか」を調べるなら、はじめのキーワードは <code>パスポート 更新 書類</code> です。開いた自治体のページに「オンライン申請」などの手続き方法が出てきたら、次の検索にその言葉を加えて絞り込めます。外務省や都道府県の公式ページなど別の資料でも答えを確かめたら、<mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">分かったことを自分の言葉で1文にまとめます。</mark> 参照した公式ページの名称もいっしょにメモしておくと、あとで見返せます。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>休日に行ける地域や日程を手がかりに舞台やイベントを探すのも、言葉や条件を入れ替えながら検索する身近な例です。 <a href="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/2026/09/20/theater-ticket-search/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">小劇場の公演をチケットサイトで探す方法｜予約フォームとの違い</a></p>

<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Google Search Tips: Choosing Keywords and Narrowing Results to Reliable Sources</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Suppose you need to dispose of a broken refrigerator and search for “refrigerator.” You will mostly see shopping sites and product reviews, leaving you unsure which page explains the actual disposal rules.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">Pick the words that matter in your question, then look at what comes up and change one word at a time.</mark></strong> This guide walks through putting your question into one sentence, refining searches with exclusion and site operators, and verifying the rules against official primary sources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 1. Put your question into one sentence"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Put your question into one sentence</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":231,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/01-question-v2.jpg" alt="An adult sits at a table with a notebook and laptop, looking toward a refrigerator and framing a disposal question" class="wp-image-231" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Looking up “refrigerators” alone mixes replacement guides with energy-saving tips. When you want disposal procedures, you need official guidance on the rules and where to request pickup. <mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">Writing down your question in one sentence first</mark> helps you judge which search results fit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your goal here is to explain in your own words where to request proper disposal of a broken refrigerator under the rules and what fees or steps are required.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 2. Choose your search keywords"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Choose your search keywords</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":232,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/02-keywords-v2.jpg" alt="Overhead view of hands arranging three icon cards for refrigerator, disposal, and checklist beside a laptop" class="wp-image-232" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The words you enter into a search engine are called keywords. From “How do I properly dispose of a broken refrigerator and what steps are needed?”, <mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">“refrigerator” names the item, while “disposal” and “how to” name what you want to know.</mark></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Type <code>refrigerator disposal how to</code> (or in Japanese, <code>冷蔵庫 処分 方法</code>) with spaces between the words. If you instead wanted to troubleshoot why your refrigerator is no longer cooling, you would start with <code>refrigerator not cooling check</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can type an entire sentence, too, yet breaking the query into a few short keywords makes it much easier to swap one word at a time when refining your search.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Keyword combinations by situation</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When your starting term is too broad, shopping pages and unrelated news fill the screen. Here is how to combine words by goal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">What you want to find</th><th scope="col">Too broad</th><th scope="col">Better starting keywords</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>How to dispose of a broken appliance legally</td><td><code>refrigerator</code></td><td><code>refrigerator disposal how to</code></td></tr><tr><td>Official procedures and required documents</td><td><code>passport</code></td><td><code>passport renewal required documents</code></td></tr><tr><td>Troubleshooting why a device stopped working</td><td><code>refrigerator broken</code></td><td><code>refrigerator not cooling check</code></td></tr><tr><td>Plays or local events you can attend on a weekend</td><td><code>stage play</code></td><td><code>Osaka small theater next month schedule</code></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<!-- /wp:html --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 3. Look at the results and change one word"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Look at the results and change one word</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":233,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/03-adjust-v2.jpg" alt="An adult comparing two search paths from a laptop: adding a keyword card to narrow retail clutter and removing an excess filter card" class="wp-image-233" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Search results are the pages your query turns up. When useful pages do not appear, look at what kind of pages are listed to decide which word to adjust next.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Mostly shopping pages or reviews? Add a word.</strong> Adding “disposal” and “how to” to <code>refrigerator</code> gives <code>refrigerator disposal how to</code> and narrows results toward disposal steps.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Too few helpful official pages? Drop a word.</strong> Searching <code>refrigerator disposal free same day</code> may surface mainly private ad listings; drop “free” and “same day” to return to <code>refrigerator disposal how to</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Spot an official term? Swap it in.</strong> In Japan, if results mention the Home Appliance Recycling Law (“家電リサイクル法”), search again with <code>冷蔵庫 家電リサイクル法 処分</code> (or “appliance recycling law refrigerator disposal”).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On Google, wrap a phrase in double quotation marks, such as <code>"Home Appliance Recycling Law"</code> (or <code>"家電リサイクル法"</code>), to match that exact phrase. To search only Japan’s Ministry of the Environment website, enter <code>冷蔵庫 処分 site:env.go.jp</code>. Here are four Google search operators worth keeping handy. <a href="https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/2466433?hl=en" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Google Search Help on refining searches</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Four helpful Google search operators</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Operator</th><th scope="col">What it does</th><th scope="col">Example query</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>"phrase"</code></td><td>Matches pages containing the exact wording in order</td><td><code>"Home Appliance Recycling Law" refrigerator</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>-word</code></td><td>Excludes shopping, ads, or unwanted topics from results</td><td><code>refrigerator disposal -shopping -sale</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>site:domain</code></td><td>Limits results to a specific website or public agency</td><td><code>冷蔵庫 処分 site:env.go.jp</code></td></tr><tr><td><code>filetype:pdf</code></td><td>Finds official leaflets, forms, or handbooks in PDF</td><td><code>appliance recycling disposal filetype:pdf</code></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark style="background-color:#fef08a;color:#422006" class="has-inline-color">Type operators (<code>site:</code>, <code>filetype:</code>, <code>-</code>) in standard ASCII with no space after the symbol.</mark> <mark style="background-color:#fee2e2;color:#991b1b" class="has-inline-color">Putting a space, such as <code>site: env.go.jp</code> or <code>- shopping</code>, keeps the filter from working properly.</mark> If nothing suitable turns up, remove one operator at a time and search again.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<!-- wp:group {"metadata":{"name":"en: 4. Check the answer with another source"},"className":"search-guide-panel","layout":{"type":"default"}} -->
<div class="wp-block-group search-guide-panel"><!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. Check the answer with another source</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":234,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/04-check-v2.jpg" alt="An adult uses a magnifying glass and notebook to compare two official guides side by side on a laptop and tablet" class="wp-image-234" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark style="background-color:#fee2e2;color:#991b1b" class="has-inline-color">Avoid settling on an answer from a search preview or AI summary alone.</mark> Open the original page and check these three points.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Does it answer your question?</strong> Here, look for clear instructions on how to hand off the refrigerator and what fees apply.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Who wrote it, and on what authority?</strong> Look for a government agency or official recycling organization. <mark style="background-color:#fef08a;color:#422006" class="has-inline-color">Check the update date for details that can change, such as fees or drop-off centers.</mark></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Do other primary sources agree?</strong> Compare multiple official pages or municipal rules. If details differ, check whether one covers household vs. commercial appliances.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pages from Japan’s Ministry of the Environment, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), and the Association for Electric Home Appliances (Home Appliance Recycling Ticket Center) consistently explain the covered items and official routes. <a href="https://www.env.go.jp/recycle/kaden/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Ministry of the Environment guide to reusing and recycling appliances (in Japanese)</a>, <a href="https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/kaden_recycle/fukyu_special/index.html" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">METI Home Appliance Recycling special page (in Japanese)</a>, and <a href="https://www.rkc.aeha.or.jp/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Association for Electric Home Appliances Recycling Ticket Center (in Japanese)</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So how should you dispose of it? <strong><mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">In Japan, household refrigerators fall under the Home Appliance Recycling Law: ask the store where you replace or originally bought it to pick it up, or follow municipal rules / bring it with a recycling ticket to a designated collection site, paying both the recycling fee and collection/transport fee.</mark></strong> The Ministry of the Environment also warns against handing appliances to unlicensed collectors advertising “free pickup.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How to tell primary sources from summary articles</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When several blog posts or comparison pages appear, trace the claim back to a primary source before acting on it. Check these three signs:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Check the publisher’s domain:</strong> Look for official domains such as <code>.go.jp</code> for Japanese ministries, local municipal domains, or the official organization or manufacturer running the rule.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Check the update date and conditions:</strong> Fees and reception desks can change. Confirm the publication year and whether the rule covers your exact case (such as household vs. commercial use).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Check for ads or promotional bias:</strong> If a page pushes a specific paid service or broker, compare its claims against official primary documentation.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></div>
<!-- /wp:group -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. Try it with a question of your own</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":235,"width":"100%","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img src="https://www.nanasinogonbei.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/05-practice-v2.jpg" alt="An adult writes a one-sentence summary and source checkmarks in a notebook beside a laptop" class="wp-image-235" style="width:100%;height:auto"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Suppose you want to know what documents are needed to renew your passport: start with <code>passport renewal documents</code>. If an official municipal page mentions “online application,” add that phrase to narrow your next search. After confirming the answer across official ministry or prefectural pages, <mark style="background-color:#dbeafe;color:#1e3a8a" class="has-inline-color">sum up what you found in one sentence in your own words.</mark> Jot down the names of the official pages you checked so you can return to them later.</p>
